Frontline Resource Allocation

Direct resources to frontline programs.

Every contribution directly backs verified field programs: primary school learning kits, community mental health training, or clean water and sanitation actions across Ghana.

Division Allocations

Where resources go.

Contributions fund real, documented frontline activity across our three core continental divisions:

  • Division 01: Education & Youth Directly purchases exercise books, stationery, and learning materials for schoolchildren in rural and underserved Ghanaian schools. →
  • Division 02: Mental Health (PAMHO) Sponsors free community mental health workshops, psychosocial counseling logistics, and public anti-stigma educational materials. →
  • Division 03: Climate & Community Funds field cleanup equipment, community sanitation drives, clean water access projects, and relief provisions. →
