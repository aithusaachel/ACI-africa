Accountability & Governance

Upholding integrity, transparent logistics, and verified impact for grantmakers and communities.

1. Transparent Field Logistics

Every financial pledge is tracked directly to verified field distributions in Ghana.

Learn more
We decline to publish fabricated statistics or make unverified claims. Every financial contribution begins as an authorized pledge, tracked directly by our Accra governance council and deployed to verified field operations.

2. Institutional Grantmaker Access

Open to bilateral MoUs, independent audits, and institutional site inspections.

Learn more
We welcome bilateral MoUs, independent audits, and programmatic site inspections with institutional funders, universities, and international development agencies.
