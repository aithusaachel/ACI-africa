Inclusive Education Across Ghana

We deliver classroom learning kits and introduce sensory pause corners for special needs pupils. Educator psychoeducation eliminates classroom ostracization.

"When teachers understand sensory processing and learning diversities, children are dignified rather than punished."
ACI Africa Field Assessment • Greater Accra School Outreach

Confronting Cultural Silence (PAMHO)

We dismantle superstitious interpretations of mental distress through community dialogues. First responders and local leaders receive psychological first-aid training.

"Stigma decreases rapidly when people realize that emotional vulnerability and mental distress affect community leaders, fathers, and youth alike."
PAMHO Community Forum Record • Greater Accra
